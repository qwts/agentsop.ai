## The four rules

- Work starts from an issue and the PR closes it: the issue holds the why.
- An agent opens its PR under its bot identity, never as the human: the log shows who acted.
- A review comment is fixed, replied to, and resolved in one pass: no thread is left half-answered.
- Before a large effort, state your understanding and recommendation, then ask how to proceed.
